Delivery Information

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Everything you need to know about how we deliver your furniture and how cash on delivery works.

1. Where we deliver

We deliver across the UK. [Confirm your exact coverage — e.g. UK mainland only, or specific cities/postcodes.]

2. How long it takes

Most orders are delivered within 24–72 hours. We confirm your delivery day with you on WhatsApp when we confirm your order. Made-to-order items may take a little longer, and we'll always tell you the timescale before you commit.

3. Delivery cost

Delivery is free anywhere we cover. There are no hidden charges added on the day.

4. Paying on delivery

  • You pay cash to the delivery team when your furniture arrives.
  • There is nothing to pay online and no deposit unless we've clearly agreed one with you beforehand.
  • Please have the agreed amount ready.

5. Check before you accept

Please inspect your item before paying and accepting it. If it's damaged or not what you ordered, tell the delivery person and contact us right away. See our Returns & Refunds Policy.

6. Missed or rearranged deliveries

If you won't be in on the agreed day, let us know as early as you can on WhatsApp and we'll rearrange a time that suits you.

7. Assembly

Assembly isn't included in the price. For some items (such as wardrobes) you can add assembly when you order; the cost is shown before you confirm.
