Your rights

Returns & Refunds

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy explains when you can cancel or return an order, and what happens if something is faulty. It sits alongside your legal rights and does not replace them.

1. Your right to cancel standard items

For most goods bought at a distance (online or by message), UK law (the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013) gives you the right to cancel within 14 days of receiving them, for any reason. To cancel, tell us within that time using the contact details below. You then return the item and we refund you.

  • The goods should be returned in their original, unused condition.
  • Unless the item was faulty or wrongly supplied, you may be responsible for the cost of returning bulky furniture.
  • We refund within 14 days of getting the goods back (or proof they were sent).
Important — made-to-order items. Furniture made or customised to your choice of colour, fabric or size is bespoke. Under the same regulations, bespoke and personalised goods are excluded from the 14-day right to cancel. Please choose colour, fabric and size carefully, and ask for a fabric photo first if you're unsure. This does not affect your rights if the item arrives faulty (see below).

2. If your item is faulty or not as described

Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, goods must be of satisfactory quality, as described, and fit for purpose. If they are not:

  • You can reject the item and get a full refund if you tell us within 30 days of delivery;
  • After 30 days, you're entitled to a repair or replacement, and a refund or price reduction if that doesn't fix it.

This applies to all items, including bespoke ones.

3. Check your furniture on delivery

Because you pay cash on delivery, please look over your item before you accept and pay. If it's clearly damaged or wrong, tell the delivery person and contact us straight away so we can put it right.

4. How to start a return or report a problem

  1. Message us on WhatsApp or email us (details below) with your name, order details and, for a fault, a photo or short video.
  2. We'll confirm the next step — return, repair, replacement or refund.
  3. We'll arrange collection or a return address for larger items.

5. Refunds

Refunds are made using the same method you paid with where possible. As orders are paid in cash on delivery, we'll agree the refund method with you when we confirm your return.
