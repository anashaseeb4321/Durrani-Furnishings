Questions

How much is delivery?

Delivery is free anywhere in the UK.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders are delivered within 24–72 hours. We confirm your delivery day on WhatsApp when we confirm your order.

How do I pay?

You pay cash on delivery. There's nothing to pay online and no card details needed.

Is assembly included?

Assembly isn't included in the price. For wardrobes you can add assembly when you order; the price depends on the width.

Do beds come with a mattress?

Only if you add one. The mattress is an optional extra on each bed, and its price is shown before you order.

Can I choose a different colour or size of sofa?

Yes. Every sofa can be made in any colour or fabric, and custom sizes are available. Tell us what you need when you order.

Where are your sofas made?

Our sofas are made in the UK.
