Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy explains what personal information Durrani Furnishings collects, how we use it, and the rights you have under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Who we are. Durrani Furnishings ("we", "us") sells sofas, beds, wardrobes and dining furniture to customers in the UK through this website and WhatsApp. We are the data controller for the information described below. Our contact details are at the bottom of this page.

1. Information we collect

When you place an order or make an enquiry, we collect:

  • Your name;
  • Your phone number (including your WhatsApp number);
  • Your delivery address;
  • Order details you send us (product, colour, size, notes);
  • Your email address, if you email us.

When you use the website, we (and the services we use) may also collect technical information such as your device type, browser, approximate location and how you interact with our pages. This is done through cookies and similar technologies — see our Cookie Policy.

2. How we use your information

  • To take, confirm and fulfil your order;
  • To arrange and complete delivery;
  • To take payment (cash on delivery);
  • To answer your questions and provide customer service;
  • To handle returns, refunds and complaints;
  • To measure and improve our advertising and website (only where you have accepted marketing cookies).

3. Our legal bases

  • Contract — to process and deliver an order you have placed.
  • Legitimate interests — to respond to enquiries and run our business.
  • Consent — for advertising and analytics cookies, which only run if you accept them.
  • Legal obligation — where we must keep records (for example, tax records).

4. Who we share it with

We only share your information where needed to run our business:

  • Delivery partners — to deliver your order.
  • WhatsApp / Meta — we use WhatsApp (owned by Meta) to receive and manage orders and enquiries. Your messages are processed by Meta under its own terms.
  • Meta advertising tools — where you accept marketing cookies, we use Meta's tools to measure and target advertising.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

5. International transfers

Some of the services we use (such as Meta / WhatsApp) may process data outside the UK, including in the USA. Where this happens, those providers rely on safeguards recognised under UK data protection law.

6. How long we keep it

We keep order information for as long as needed to complete your order and to meet our legal and accounting duties, then delete or anonymise it. Enquiries that don't lead to an order are kept only as long as needed to deal with them.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong;
  • Ask us to delete your information;
  • Object to, or restrict, how we use it;
  • Withdraw consent for cookies or marketing at any time.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
